# Smart Invoice Offline Mode

## What is Offline Mode?

Offline Mode allows you to use Smart Invoice's AI text features directly on your device, without needing an internet connection or making API calls to our servers. This gives you more privacy, reduced latency in some cases, and ensures basic functionality even when offline.

## Benefits of Offline Mode

- **Reduced API costs**: Process text-based AI tasks on your device
- **Privacy**: Your data stays local for text processing
- **Offline use**: Continue using core features without internet
- **Faster responses**: For simple queries, local processing can be faster

## How to Enable Offline Mode

1. Navigate to your **Profile** screen
2. Find the **Offline AI Processing** toggle
3. Switch it to the ON position
4. Wait for the one-time download to complete (~10-15MB)
5. You're all set! A small "Offline Mode" indicator will appear when active

## What Works in Offline Mode

- **Chat responses**: Basic financial queries and conversations
- **Text summarization**: Condensing text into key points
- **Simple classifications**: Basic categorization of text content

## What Requires Online Mode

- **Image processing**: Scanning receipts and invoices
- **Complex financial analysis**: Detailed predictions and recommendations
- **Multi-turn reasoning**: Complex, context-heavy conversations

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much space does it use?
The offline model requires about 10-15MB of storage space, downloaded once and cached for future use.

### Will it drain my battery?
The offline model uses minimal power during short interactions. Extended use may have a small impact on battery life, but generally less than streaming video.

### Is the quality the same as online mode?
No, the offline model is optimized for size and speed, so responses won't be as comprehensive or nuanced as those from our full Gemini API. It's best for simple interactions.

### How do I switch back to online mode?
Simply toggle the "Offline AI Processing" switch to the OFF position in your Profile settings.

### Can I use offline mode on mobile?
Yes! The offline mode works on mobile browsers that support WebAssembly (most modern browsers).

### Is my data still saved when using offline mode?
Yes, your data is still stored in your account. The offline mode only changes where the AI processing happens.

## Troubleshooting

### The download seems stuck
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page and enabling offline mode again
- Make sure you have enough storage space on your device

### Responses are very slow
- The first response after enabling offline mode may take 1-3 seconds
- If all responses are slow, try restarting your browser
- Some devices may not have enough processing power for optimal performance

### The quality of responses is poor
- The offline model is optimized for size, not quality
- For important or complex queries, consider switching to online mode
- Try providing more context in your questions

### The offline indicator doesn't appear
- Try toggling offline mode off and on again
- Restart your browser
- Check if you have any browser extensions that might be interfering
